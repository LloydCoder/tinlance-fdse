# TSIC integration

FDSE consumes canonical TSIC integration contracts while retaining authority over delivery-domain semantics.

- **TSIC** owns ecosystem integration and certification.
- **FDSE** owns engagement semantics, evidence, outcomes and route selection.
- **Engineering** and **Transformation** are explicit first-class routes.
- **Agent Platform** owns consequential execution authority.

The delivery handoff carries tenant, account, signal, evidence, technical/business hypothesis, confidence, urgency and explicit `delivery_route`.

The CI conformance gate verifies identity, event, delivery, trace and economic-attribution contracts plus the route and authority invariants.

## Acquisition handoff

The canonical engineering opportunity carries `account_id`, `signal_id`, `evidence_id`, `tenant_id`, `technical_hypothesis`, `business_hypothesis`, `confidence`, `urgency`, `decision_maker`, and explicit `delivery_route`.

`delivery_route=engineering` enters the engineering/FDE path. `delivery_route=transformation` enters the PE-style transformation path. The routes share governed execution, evidence, trace and economic attribution contracts but do not share domain semantics.
