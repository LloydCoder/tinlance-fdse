## Final certification boundary

FDSE can validate the completeness and consistency of supplied evidence. It must not manufacture evidence or self-authorize production readiness. External systems remain authoritative for runtime health, deployment, customer environment readiness, approvals, and operational controls.

## Final forensic requirement

A release is not represented as production-certified merely because the repository CI is green. Enterprise certification requires the external evidence matrix to be populated and verified by the applicable systems.
